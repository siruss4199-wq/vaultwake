## Suggested listing
**Title:** Vaultwake Merge

**Genre:** Casual / Merge / Puzzle / Adventure

**Short description**

Merge unusual coastal treasures, fulfill crew orders, and restore a storm-worn shoreline. Discover rare parts and unlock the secrets of the Vault.

**Long description**

A storm has scattered the tools, maps, and machinery of a coastal outpost. In Vaultwake Merge, combine matching items to craft better equipment, fulfill crew requests for coins, and complete special Gold orders to gather restoration materials. Rebuild the shoreline piece by piece, uncover sealed cargo, discover rarer components, and open the mysterious Vault as the story expands. Play at your own pace and return to the same camp in your browser.

**How to play**

1. Tap a generator to produce a part (costs energy).
2. Drag two identical parts together to merge them into a higher-level one.
3. Complete crew orders to earn coins. Gold story orders grant restoration materials and some coins.
4. Open the coast view and use the required materials and coins to rebuild locations.
5. Discover rare parts, unlock sealed cargo, and improve your generators.

**Controls**

Mouse: click generators/buttons and drag matching parts together.

Touch: tap generators/buttons and drag matching parts together.

**Grounded supported features:** 6×6 merge board, multiple narrated coastal areas, rare extras, locally saved progress, energy-based crafting. No multiplayer, cloud sync, offline app, leaderboard or guaranteed cross-device saves.
