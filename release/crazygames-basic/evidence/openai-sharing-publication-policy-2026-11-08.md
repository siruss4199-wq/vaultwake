## Relevance to Vaultwake Merge

- The policy is written mainly for **text** published from the API and for posts on social media. The game's ChatGPT output is **images** made in ChatGPT, not text from the API. The provisions do not map one-to-one, but the two principles apply in spirit:
  - **Attribute the work to the owner.**
  - **Disclose that AI was used.** Do not present the art as human-made.
- The binding rule that does apply is in the OpenAI **Terms of Use** (read directly on 2026-11-08, `RIGHTS_REPORT.md` §2): you may not represent Output as human-generated when it is not.
- **No restriction found** on commercial publication of the images, on CrazyGames or elsewhere.
- No provision requires a credit in a particular place, but a disclosure the player can find is the safe way to meet both texts.

## Recommended wording

**Credits (in the portal's credits or description field):**

> Vaultwake Merge by [DEVELOPER NAME]. Artwork was created with AI tools (OpenAI's ChatGPT and
> Anthropic's Claude Code) and selected, edited and assembled by [DEVELOPER NAME], who takes
> responsibility for the published game.

**One-line disclosure at the end of the full description:**

> Made with the help of AI tools: the game's artwork was created with ChatGPT and Claude Code and
> edited by the developer.

Both lines are true to the owner's statements (`owner-confirmation-2026-11-08.md`). Neither says the art
is hand-drawn.
