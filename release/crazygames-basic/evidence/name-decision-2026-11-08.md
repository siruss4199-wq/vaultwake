## The naming risks to review

From `RIGHTS_REPORT.md` §3. These come from web searches on 2026-11-08; no trademark office was searched.

- **Exact title:** no game named "Vaultwake" or "Vaultwake Merge" was found on the web, Steam or the app stores.
- **Same word, other field:** `miller-joe/vaultwake` on GitHub is Vault-related software, not a game.
- **Similar game names:**
  - *Battlewake* (Survios, a VR naval game).
  - *Merge Ghost – Ghost Vault*.
  - *Vault Merge Coin Stacker*.
  - A "Vault" merge app listing.
- **"MERGE …" marks:** Metacore Games Oy holds MERGE MANSION and MERGE VIKINGS, and there are several US "MERGE" word-mark filings. "Merge" is a generic genre word, but owners of "MERGE + word" marks may object to close imitation.
