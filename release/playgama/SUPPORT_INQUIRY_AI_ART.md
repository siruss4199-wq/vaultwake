**What the game is.** It is a merge game with an original design: a storm-wrecked coast restored across 6 chapters and more than 50 places, each in three stages. **How the visuals were made:**

- What a person (the developer) did: directed every chapter and place, chose the images from many generated ones, rejected some visual directions, playtested on real phones and had them fixed, and approved the final art.
- Sound is recorded CC0 audio from Kenney and OpenGameArt. The interface icons were drawn for the game.
