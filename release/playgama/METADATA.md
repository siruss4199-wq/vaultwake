## Short description (saved)
Vaultwake Merge is a cozy merge puzzle on a storm-wrecked coast. Tap generators for parts, drag twins together to make better
tools, fill crew orders for coins and materials, and rebuild the beach camp, the cliff worksite and the hidden harbour stage by
stage while the story of the sealed Vault door unfolds.

## How to play (saved)
Tap a generator to make a part (1 energy). Drag a part onto an identical one to merge it into the next level. Deliver what a
crew order asks for to earn coins; the gold order also brings the materials for the next stage. Tap the strip under the orders
to go to the coast and press Build when a stage has its materials and coins. Energy refills over time. Controls: mouse click
and drag on desktop, tap and drag on touch screens.

## Long description (for the catalogue page and other portals)
A storm has wrecked the coast, and a crew has set up camp below an old Vault door in the cliffs. Rebuild it, one place at a
time.

Tap Rivet Rover and the other generators for parts, then drag two identical parts together to merge them into something
better: small parts into finer tools, shells into treasures. Crew members post orders for the parts they need. Fill
them for coins, and fill the gold order for the materials of the next stage. Spend both on the coast: the path, the camp
tent, the workshop and the signal mast change in front of you, three stages each, and every stage tells a little more of
the story.

When the beach is done, the Survey Worksite opens up the cliff, then the Vault itself, the Silent Observatory inside it and the
Hidden Harbour beyond its sea gate. New generators arrive as yours level up; sealed cargo, tideglass and rare extras give the
board something to solve. A guided first loop takes you to your first build, and when the board fills up the game points at a
move that frees a space.

- Merge-2 on a 7×7 board, with a shed for four parts off the board
- Crew orders, a gold order per stage, coins and materials
- Six chapters of coast to restore, each place in three stages
- Energy that refills over time; a daily gift; optional rewarded ads for energy, a crate or a new order
- Plays in portrait and landscape, with touch or mouse; progress saved automatically

## Developer note (`story`, draft for the owner to edit and save)
Vaultwake Merge started as a small board of parts and a beach that needed rebuilding, and the coast grew one place at a time
from there. The first few minutes took the most rework. Early versions left new players on a busy board with no idea what to
do, so the game now walks you to your first build and then steps back. A full board was another sore spot, and now the game
points at a move that frees a space. Which place on the coast did you build first?
